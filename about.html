ABOUT THE PROJECT

JobAdda

A student-focused job, training and placement portal built as a practical college web development project.

Why JobAdda?

Students need a simple place to discover internships, entry-level jobs and career development opportunities. JobAdda brings these opportunities together and helps students prepare for their professional journey.

Project objectives

Easy discovery Search for jobs and internships
Student dashboard Track saved jobs and applications
Placement preparation Develop skills and prepare for interviews

Technology

HTML5 for page structure, CSS3 for responsive design and JavaScript for interactive application features.

A basic demonstration can run in the browser. Features such as accounts, messaging and real job applications require additional implementation.

More Than a Job Portal

Inspired by professional networking platforms such as LinkedIn, JobAdda aims to help students build career skills, discover opportunities, connect with the professional world and prepare for campus placements.

Training and Skills

Discover technical learning resources, skill development programs, aptitude practice and interview preparation.

Jobs and Internships

Explore fresher jobs, internships and entry-level opportunities to gain practical experience and begin your career.

Placement Support

Prepare resumes, practice interview questions, improve communication skills and organize job applications.
